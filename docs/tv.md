Use Buoy to inspect React Native TV apps from Desktop. TV integration runs headless so the tool menu does not enter the app's D-pad focus order.

## Getting Started

- [Overview](./tv/overview) — why TV runs headless, and what is verified today
- [Quick Start](./tv/quick-start) — connect a TV app to Buoy Desktop
- [Installation](./tv/installation) — requirements, packages, host tooling, devices, license

## TV tools

- [TV Remote](./tools/tv-remote) — press the D-pad from your desktop, record and replay macros
- [Focus Inspector](./tools/focus-inspector) — what holds D-pad focus, and why it gets stuck

See [TV compatibility](./tv/overview#the-rest-of-the-suite) before adding other tools. Their setup may require stores or providers from your app.
